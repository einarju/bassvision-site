# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Electronic music labels releasing on underground scenes (techno, warehouse, dark house, psytrance). Independent artists who need professional visual production for releases and live sets. Bookers and promoters who want branded visual identities. They are on YouTube at 2am watching demos, forwarding links to their label manager, deciding in seconds whether the studio has the right aesthetic.

## Product Purpose

Studio portfolio + service offering + direct-download store. Converts visitors into clients (label retainer or one-off visualizer commission) and buyers of VJ loop packs. Success looks like: a label manager books a retainer call, or an artist downloads the free pack and upgrades to the paid one.

## Brand Personality

Precise · Cinematic · Underground. Not lo-fi bedroom DIY — the opposite: the feeling of a high-end production house that operates underground. Expensive-feeling without being corporate. The work should look like it was made by someone who has taste and charges for it. Three words: **disciplined, dark, cinematic**.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages (hero metric template, icon+heading+text card grids, SaaS-cream color palette)
- Neon-on-black rave / club flyer aesthetics (obvious "music = neon" reflex)
- Bright colorful electronic music merch sites
- Template portfolio sites with centered stacks and uniform card grids
- Glassmorphism, gradient text, animated background particles
- Anything that reads as "web designer's portfolio" rather than "visual production studio"

## Design Principles

1. **Work speaks first.** Every visual decision defers to the work being shown. The site is a frame, not a subject.
2. **Underground does not mean cheap.** Darkness and restraint must read as intentional luxury, not low effort.
3. **No ornament without function.** Every element earns its place or it goes.
4. **Committed to the scene.** Typography, color, and motion should feel native to the underground electronic music world — not designed by an outsider looking in.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Prefers-reduced-motion respected. Sufficient contrast on dark backgrounds (aim for 4.5:1 for body text).
